# Source and Practice-Question Policy

Status: Reviewed policy

Last reviewed: 2026-08-16

## Purpose

This repository preserves technical learning without exposing confidential exam content or republishing third-party training material. The policy applies to conversation notes, screenshots, course exports, practice exams, remembered questions, and code derived from learning resources.

## Allowed

- Original explanations written from independently understood concepts and verified with primary sources.
- Original scenario-based practice questions created specifically for this repository.
- Original code and synthetic datasets with clear setup, validation, and teardown instructions.
- Short attributed references and links to official documentation.
- Links to retired sample questions that Databricks publishes in its official exam guide.
- Personal learning reflections that do not reveal exam content or private program information.

## Not allowed

- Questions or answer choices remembered or reconstructed from a live certification exam.
- Exam dumps, answer keys, screenshots, recordings, or score reports.
- Paid Udemy, Whizlabs, Databricks Academy, or partner-training questions and course exports.
- Third-party notebooks, images, archives, wheels, or datasets without a license that permits republication.
- Customer, employer, Partner Portal, or internal Databricks information.
- Rewriting a protected question while retaining its distinctive scenario, choices, or answer logic.

## Safe transformation workflow

1. Identify the underlying technical capability without copying the source wording.
2. Confirm the capability is still relevant using the current official exam guide and product documentation.
3. Write a fresh production scenario from first principles using synthetic names and data.
4. Explain assumptions, alternatives, failure modes, security, observability, performance, and cost.
5. Add runnable evidence or mark the example as illustrative.
6. Cite primary sources and record the review date.
7. Keep the legacy source private when its license or origin is uncertain.

## Original practice-question format

Every future practice scenario should contain:

- the capability being assessed;
- a production context and explicit constraints;
- one best decision or implementation;
- an explanation of why it fits the constraints;
- credible alternatives and why they were not selected;
- a validation or experiment where practical; and
- links to current primary sources.

The objective is to demonstrate engineering judgment, not to predict or reproduce exam items.
